# Retirement review

Educational review context, not a financial instruction.

Separate accumulation from withdrawal planning. Show the actual nominal/real, return, inflation, contribution and horizon assumptions used by the engine. A constant-return path does not test withdrawal sequence risk or establish a safe withdrawal rate. Ask for retirement spending, pension income and dates rather than invent benefits. Source: nism_retirement.

Source metadata and verification dates: knowledge/finance_sources.json. Facts and calculations must come from the user records and deterministic services.
