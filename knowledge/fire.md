# Financial independence planning

Start with spending in today's rupees, a chosen age and a planning horizon; inflate spending to the target date.
Subtract only explicitly stated, correctly timed pension income. Zero pension must be confirmed rather than inferred.
Compare a withdrawal-rate scenario with a finite-horizon zero-real-return spending cross-check; neither proves safety.
Work backwards using earmarked assets and month-end contribution mathematics under stated return assumptions.
Bound contributions by both an agreed monthly investment budget and recorded cashflow capacity after other commitments.
Psychometric willingness and adaptive loss sensitivity bound allocation alternatives; a funding gap cannot raise risk tolerance.
Compare dated eligible bank FD terms for the deposit slice; today's quote does not price future bookings or renewals.
Earmark money once; retain the reserve, other goals, missing evidence, cost/tax limits and sequence risk.
