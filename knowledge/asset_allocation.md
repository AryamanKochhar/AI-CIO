# Asset allocation

Educational review context, not a financial instruction.

Allocation concerns broad exposure and its fit with the recorded policy. Portfolio value and monthly contribution are different quantities. Unknown fund look-through stays unclassified. Do not derive target percentages from age or occupation alone. Compare changes only with consistent horizon, liquidity, cost and tax assumptions. Educational source: cfa_portfolio_planning.

Source metadata and verification dates: knowledge/finance_sources.json. Facts and calculations must come from the user records and deterministic services.
