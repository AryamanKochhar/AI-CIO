# Diversification and rebalancing

Educational review context, not a financial instruction.

Product count does not establish exposure diversity. Review issuer/sector overlap only where actual holdings support it. Distinguish contribution-led changes from sales, and explain their different tax, liquidity and cost trade-offs without selecting a universal winner. A policy band is not a promised risk bound.
