# Liquidity and deposit context

Educational review context, not a financial instruction.

Compare accessible funds with identified essential outgoings and dated commitments. Do not count earmarked cash twice or assume all deposits can be withdrawn without cost. Reserve months are planning assumptions, not a universal minimum. Insurance eligibility requires insured-bank identity and combined ownership/capacity details; no per-FD protection inference. Sources: dicgc_guide; cfa_portfolio_planning.

Source metadata and verification dates: knowledge/finance_sources.json. Facts and calculations must come from the user records and deterministic services.
