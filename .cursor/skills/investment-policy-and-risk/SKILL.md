# Investment policy and risk capacity

Primary sources: cfa_suitability, cfa_portfolio_planning and sebi_adviser in knowledge/finance_sources.json. Professional and educational standards inform this research product; they do not establish registration, compliance certification or current tax rates.

## Establish the policy context
Identify the decision objective, goal dates and priorities, cash needs, liabilities, loss-bearing resources and restrictions. Record what is user-provided, imported, derived or unknown. Age, job title, household type and questionnaire willingness are insufficient to establish risk capacity.

## Keep three risk questions separate
Willingness concerns comfort with uncertainty. Capacity concerns whether losses or illiquidity compromise commitments. Required return describes a goal assumption, not permission to take more risk. When these conflict, show the conflict and review timing, contribution, spending or goals using available deterministic scenarios.

## Constrain review alternatives
A concentration observation should connect to the actual goal or cash constraint, with denominator and valuation date. Compare only supported alternatives; explain liquidity, tax, costs and reversibility. The next action should identify the relevant screen or missing fact. Do not output a personalized trade order.

## Preserve evidence limits
A service threshold is an application policy unless an identified source establishes otherwise. A forward scenario or normalized priority share is not a probability of success. Revisit a policy after relevant life, goal or funding changes; do not impose a universal schedule. Cite only supplied source IDs and label educational principles separately from user facts. Empty evidence permits empty finding arrays and an explicit request for missing information.
