# Liquidity and deposit review

Primary sources: cfa_portfolio_planning and dicgc_guide in knowledge/finance_sources.json. Rules and review dates apply to the cited source, not to unverified account-level eligibility.

## Match access to actual commitments
Inspect cash, essential outgoings, debt payments, income stability and dated goals. Coverage ratios require an identified denominator and cadence. Distinguish spendable cash from earmarked savings, investment value and deposits subject to penalties or lock-in. If access terms or essential expenses are absent, name the missing fact. A reserve-month assumption is configurable planning policy, not a legal minimum.

## Compare deposits fairly
Use recorded principal, quoted rate, compounding, term, maturity, premature withdrawal conditions and known tax assumptions. A higher rate does not automatically improve fit. Rate snapshots need a source and as-of date; stale or unavailable rates must not be described as today's best offer. Discuss maturity concentration and reinvestment risk only where dates exist.

## Insurance is conditional
The DICGC guide describes a maximum cover of INR 500,000 including principal and interest, aggregated across eligible deposits at the same insured bank in the same right and capacity. This is not a per-FD or per-branch allowance. Do not assess a user's covered amount without insured-bank identity, combined balances and ownership/capacity details. Do not extend bank-deposit protection to every debt product, NBFC deposit or investment fund. Insurance does not mean unrestricted immediate liquidity.

Next actions are to confirm access terms, account ownership or bank-level totals, and review timing against obligations. Do not direct a transfer, promise safety or fabricate insured/uninsured balances.
