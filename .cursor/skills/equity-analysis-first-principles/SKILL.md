# Equity research: evidence before interpretation

These are original project summaries, not quotations from the books or complete course material. Source metadata and actual dated observations remain separate. Publisher book listings establish bibliography only; complete copyrighted books are not bundled.

## Separate price from value
A market price or technical indicator is not fundamental value. Require dated comparable company filings and explicit valuation assumptions; missing earnings, cashflows or ownership evidence remains not evaluated.

## Explain business mechanisms
Compare profitability, leverage, liquidity and cash generation over comparable periods and relevant peers. A single ratio or a one-day sector move cannot establish the investment case.

## Respect portfolio constraints
An equity observation remains conditional on the Twin horizon, liquidity, existing look-through exposure and ability and willingness to bear loss. A framework checklist does not create permission to trade.
