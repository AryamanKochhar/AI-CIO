# Tax: determine scope before calculating

These are original project summaries, not quotations from the books or complete course material. Source metadata and actual dated observations remain separate. Publisher book listings establish bibliography only; complete copyrighted books are not bundled.

## Establish period and classification
Mutual fund tax classification requires verified scheme attributes and acquisition and disposal dates; a fund name or debt label is insufficient. The Income-tax Act 2025 applies to the relevant new income periods, while prior periods remain under the earlier Act; verify subsequent amendments and transitional definitions.

## Withhold unsupported switch estimates
Do not apply a blanket short-term rule to every debt fund or assume every other mutual fund is equity-oriented. A proposed switch is not the same as current-year realized activity. Withhold an amount when exact lots, applicable class, sale scope, exit terms or supported rule-period coverage is absent.

## Reconcile before tax optimization
Reconcile statements, tax lots and AIS records before discussing realization or loss set-off. Withholding and an unrealized valuation change are not final tax liability. No transaction should be described as tax-free because a field is missing.
