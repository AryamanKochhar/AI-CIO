# Deposits: compare enforceable cashflows

These are original project summaries, not quotations from the books or complete course material. Source metadata and actual dated observations remain separate. Publisher book listings establish bibliography only; complete copyrighted books are not bundled.

## Compare the actual deposit contract
Compare deposits over the same eligible term, payout basis and booking date. A card rate is not an after-tax yield, early-access promise or future renewal rate. Use the dated official FD catalogue and source terms; never copy a model rate.

## Distinguish deposits from fund units
A debt mutual fund is not a bank fixed deposit and does not receive deposit insurance merely because its holdings include debt. Deposit insurance depends on bank identity, depositor right and capacity and aggregated eligible balances.

## Match liquidity before headline yield
Match maturity and withdrawal conditions to recorded cash needs. Verify premature closure penalties and withholding separately from final tax, and keep unknown terms visible.
