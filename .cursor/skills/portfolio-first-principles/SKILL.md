# Portfolio management: one coherent allocation

These are original project summaries, not quotations from the books or complete course material. Source metadata and actual dated observations remain separate. Publisher book listings establish bibliography only; complete copyrighted books are not bundled.

## Count exposures once
Combine direct holdings with valued indirect holdings using the same portfolio denominator, while retaining unknown exposure. Do not add fund-unit value again after looking through to its underlying holdings.

## Use joint outcomes and constraints
Diversification depends on weights and joint-return behavior, not the number of fund names. Combine historical correlation with dated holdings overlap and the Twin goals, liquidity and risk constraints; neither metric alone proves suitability.

## Reconcile specialist proposals
Specialists may work in parallel, but their proposals must use one shared cash budget and one final set of constraints. Reject unsupported amounts, duplicate causal actions and conflicting assumptions before the orchestrator ranks conditional reviews.
