# NISM investment-adviser foundations

Educational source: existing NISM Series X-A workbook under docs/books; public objectives: nism_l1_objectives in knowledge/finance_sources.json. This original guide is not a workbook reproduction, qualification or current-law rule table.

## Review financial capacity before asset selection
Read recorded income cadence, necessary outgoings, loan payments, liquidity and committed goals. A high psychometric risk score does not establish capacity for loss. Missing household commitments, insurance and income stability remain explicit gaps. Do not derive these from age, occupation or a persona label.

## Prioritise goals without counting assets twice
For each goal, inspect target date, amount basis, essential/discretionary status, current earmarked assets and contribution assumptions. Ask which goals are flexible before discussing trade-offs. The same cash cannot independently fund every goal; overlapping funding is a limitation unless the engine implements joint allocation. A shortfall is not solved merely by assuming a higher return.

## Review options and next step
Use software-provided surplus, coverage and projection results. Compare options such as revisiting contribution, timing or discretionary target, with each option conditional on known capacity. State which inputs would change the assessment. Send the user to the recorded goal or finance field that needs review; avoid a vague instruction to invest more.

Do not assume a universal reserve size, allocation percentage or product eligibility. Mark illustrative service assumptions as assumptions. Report observed facts, why they matter, constrained review options, trade-offs, a specific next step, sources and missing data.
