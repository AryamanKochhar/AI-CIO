# Mutual funds: identify, compare and explain

These are original project summaries, not quotations from the books or complete course material. Source metadata and actual dated observations remain separate. Publisher book listings establish bibliography only; complete copyrighted books are not bundled.

## Establish exact scheme identity
A scheme name alone does not establish its ISIN, Direct or Regular plan, Growth or IDCW option, benchmark or tax classification. Resolve exact identity first; ambiguous matches remain unresolved.

## Compare matched total returns
Compare a fund and its appropriate total-return benchmark over the same dates and return basis. NAV returns are already net of recurring fund expenses; do not subtract TER again. IDCW needs distribution adjustments. Investor XIRR depends on dated cashflows and is different from a fund CAGR.

## Review persistence before action
One weak return window does not establish persistent underperformance or justify a sale. Review multiple supported windows, rolling consistency, mandate, risk and costs; overlapping windows are not independent tests. Any review thresholds are application policy, not regulator-prescribed sell rules.

## Separate overlap from correlation
Holdings overlap measures common disclosed exposure; return correlation measures historical co-movement. Weighted overlap is the sum of the smaller disclosed weights for each common security, without renormalizing partial disclosures. Record disclosure dates and unknown weight. Correlation needs aligned return observations and sufficient nonzero variance; it does not prove future diversification.

## Compare costs within the same mandate
A lower TER does not by itself establish a suitable replacement fund. Compare equivalent exposure, plan and option. Gross TER differences are not promised net savings: exit charges, tax lots and switching costs must be established. Unknown costs stay unknown.

## Use the Twin constraints
A fund review must consider the recorded horizon, liquidity, loss capacity and declared risk willingness together. A high questionnaire score cannot fund an unaffordable SIP, while a low score alone does not identify which scheme to sell. Keep shared contributions within the agreed budget and do not infer suitability from age or persona labels.

## Preserve unavailable evidence
Missing benchmark, holdings disclosure, tax classification or transactions means that dimension is not evaluated. Do not turn unavailable data into zero overlap, zero tax, a neutral return or a portfolio health probability. A partial disclosure provides a measured lower bound rather than full-portfolio overlap.
