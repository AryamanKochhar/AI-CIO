# Retirement planning and withdrawal risk

Educational source: existing NISM Series XVII workbook under docs/books and nism_retirement public objectives. Use the source manifest for scope and review date. This is an original applied guide, not a fund-selection or withdrawal-rate guarantee.

## Separate accumulation from withdrawals
Identify the retirement date, spending basis, expected pension or other income, earmarked assets and whether withdrawals have begun. Do not invent EPF, gratuity, pension, healthcare coverage or partner income. Occupation and household labels do not supply those facts.

## State the projection basis
Use the deterministic engine's contribution timing, return, inflation, horizon and tax/cost assumptions. A nominal corpus is not purchasing power. Never silently subtract inflation twice or combine assumptions from different engines. If inflation is absent, label the purchasing-power question unevaluated. Longevity and healthcare needs require user context, not a fixed lifespan assumption presented as fact.

## Explain sequence risk accurately
With withdrawals, the order of returns can alter outcomes even when average returns match. A constant-return accumulation path does not evaluate that risk. Do not call scenario bands confidence intervals or infer a safe withdrawal percentage from them. Show the limitation and suggest reviewing withdrawal timing, essential spending and liquid resources.

Compare only supported changes to saving, retirement timing or target spending. Explain the effect on current liquidity and other goals. If inputs are incomplete, the next step is to complete retirement spending/date/income fields. No claim of fully funded retirement, guaranteed pension or best NPS manager is supported by educational material alone.
