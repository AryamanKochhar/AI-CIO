# Goals and retirement: fund the spending liability

These are original project summaries, not quotations from the books or complete course material. Source metadata and actual dated observations remain separate. Publisher book listings establish bibliography only; complete copyrighted books are not bundled.

## Tie assumptions to the spending plan
Define spending in today or future rupees explicitly, then model inflation, income, longevity and affordable contributions on the same basis. Historical fund performance is not a forecast input unless the user chooses an explicit scenario.

## Separate preference and capacity
Declared questionnaire preferences are inputs to willingness, not a complete loss-capacity assessment. Use the lower of an agreed investment budget and uncommitted cashflow capacity; avoid allocating the same assets or contribution twice.

## Show scenario limits
A deterministic corpus path does not measure sequence risk or probability of success. A funding shortfall calls for budget, timing or spending review, not an automatic increase in market risk.
