# Growth-stock framework: evidence gate

Project source: existing CANSLIM Strict Mode Handbook under docs/books. It is an educational framework, not a validated prediction model, regulator standard or current-price feed. No official source in the manifest endorses this framework.

## Evidence gate
A letter-labelled checklist is not a score until the required observations exist. Earnings growth needs dated comparable reported figures; leadership needs an identified benchmark and period; price/volume patterns need a suitable historical series; ownership and supply claims need reliable dated records. Statement holdings and one sector day-change do not supply these inputs.

## Unsupported case
If fundamental, price or volume evidence is absent, report the relevant dimensions as not evaluated. Do not infer leadership from a popular name, growth from a sector label or institutional sponsorship from fund ownership. Never invent an entry price, stop, breakout, market direction, pass/fail score or target return.

## Supported educational comparison
Explain the supplied observation, its period and its limitation. Any concentrated growth-style review remains subordinate to the user's horizon, liquidity, loss capacity and policy constraints. A positive framework observation does not establish suitability or override tax/cost concerns.

Output a source-bounded checklist and missing inputs, followed by a review action such as inspecting the actual evidence. Do not rank securities, issue buy/sell orders or represent a technical pattern as a calibrated success probability.
