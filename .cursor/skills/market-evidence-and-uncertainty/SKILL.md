# Market evidence and uncertainty

Sources: cfa_covariance, sec_allocation and sebi_investment_constraints in knowledge/finance_sources.json. Original condensed concepts, not copied full texts.

## Identify what was measured
Price return measures price change over stated dates; total return also accounts for distributions. A price indicator does not establish fundamental value. Price change alone supplies no earnings, cashflow or executable fill. Specify corporate-action adjustments, missing observations, currency and date. Do not claim intraday freshness from completed daily candles.

## Explain diversification through joint outcomes
For two assets, variance = w1^2*s1^2 + w2^2*s2^2 + 2*w1*w2*s1*s2*rho. The covariance term explains why counting holdings is insufficient. Portfolio risk requires both weights and joint-return evidence. Do not invent correlations from sector names or assume historical dependence stays fixed in a crisis. Missing look-through and unvalued assets limit the denominator.

## Do not convert a ranking into a forecast
Application support shares rank reviews; they are not calibrated probabilities. A scenario is conditional on its inputs. Return order, loss magnitude and recovery time are separate questions. After a fractional loss d below 1, recovery to the prior level requires d/(1-d), before fees or cashflows. This identity does not predict a recovery. Independent scenarios are not mutually exclusive outcomes whose labels establish probabilities.

## Turn uncertainty into the next question
State the missing observation that could change the decision. Prefer a specific request for comparable earnings, cost disclosure, investment budget or goal deadline to another generic concentration warning. Reuse a canonical review rather than rephrasing it across specialist panels.
