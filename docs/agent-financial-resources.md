# Financial resources for specialist agents

Each specialist receives its own resource pack and the shared portfolio guide. The orchestrator records what was supplied, then separately validates the exact principles that an accepted finding actually applied. Resource availability is not proof of application.

## Resource matrix

### Equity Agent

- [Research Analyst: Test Objectives, effective January 20, 2026](https://www.nism.ac.in/test-objectives-research-analyst-w-e-f-january-20-2026) (`equity_nism_research`): Public objectives: research purpose, company and financial analysis, valuation and risk. The page HTML title mentions 2021 but the content heading identifies January 2026.
- [Financial Analysis Techniques](https://www.cfainstitute.org/insights/professional-learning/refresher-readings/2026/financial-analysis-techniques) (`equity_cfa_financial`): Public summary supports explicit purpose, comparable ratios and explanation of mechanisms; no single ratio establishes company quality.
- [Equity Valuation: Concepts and Basic Tools](https://www.cfainstitute.org/insights/professional-learning/refresher-readings/2026/equity-valuation-concepts-basic-tools) (`equity_cfa_valuation`): Public summary of valuation approaches and assumptions; a market price or indicator alone is not estimated intrinsic value.
- [Corporate Filings: Equity](https://www.nseindia.com/companies-listing/corporate-filings-application?id=equity) (`equity_nse_filings`): Official exchange filing access, not a claim any specific company filing was retrieved or validated. Actual recommendations need dated filing evidence.
- [How to Make Money in Stocks, Fourth Edition](https://www.mheducation.com/highered/mhp/product/how-make-money-stocks-winning-system-good-times-bad-fourth-edition.html) (`book_oneil_stocks`): Publisher bibliographic/catalogue context only. The full copyrighted book is not bundled or claimed read; CANSLIM is an educational framework and requires actual supporting data.
- [Preserving the Wealth](https://www.cfainstitute.org/insights/professional-learning/refresher-readings/2026/preserving-the-wealth) (`cfa_wealth_risks`): Purchasing power, human capital, longevity and withdrawal sequencing; public summary only, not the gated full reading.

### Mutual Fund Agent

- [Mutual Fund Distributors: Test Objectives](https://www.nism.ac.in/test-objectives-mutual-fund-distributors) (`mf_nism_distributors`): Public objectives units 10-12: returns, risk, appropriate benchmarks, tracking and investor needs; not the complete workbook.
- [Master Circular for Mutual Funds](https://www.sebi.gov.in/legal/master-circulars/mar-2026/master-circular-for-mutual-funds_100491.html) (`mf_sebi_master_2026`): Applicable Indian MF framework and disclosures; verify subsequent circulars and the relevant paragraph before legal claims. - [Standard III(C): Suitability](https://www.cfainstitute.org/standards/professionals/code-ethics-standards/standards-of-practice-iii-c) (`cfa_suitability`): Whole-portfolio suitability, client constraints and review discipline; not Indian legal authority.
- [The Little Book of Common Sense Investing, updated edition](https://www.wiley.com/en-us/the-little-book-of-common-sense-investing-the-only-way-to-guarantee-your-fair-share-of-stock-market-returns-updated-and-revised-p-9781119404507) (`book_bogle_common_sense`): Bibliographic context only: 2017, ISBN 9781119404507. Publisher automated fetch blocked; complete book unavailable. Do not cite metadata as verification of book passages or investment outcomes.

### Tax Agent

## Evidence boundaries

The six primary source families are public regulator guidance, legislation, professional curricula, official product terms, exchange disclosures and published original research. The two book records are bibliographic context: the complete books are not bundled, no page quotes are invented, and book publisher metadata is not used as text proof. Wiley access is restricted; the Bogle bibliography is retained with that limitation.

The March 2026 SEBI MF circular is scoped to its applicable framework, with later circular verification required. The tax pack includes the Income-tax Act 2025 as amended by Finance Act 2026 and transition guidance. The capital-gains education page still uses 1961 Act references and is expressly labelled as historical/conceptual context. It cannot establish a current tax rate by itself.

[Retrieval receipts](validation/agent-resources.json) retain response hashes and access status without copying full copyrighted works. A direct HTTP block and a successful browser reader result are recorded separately. A retrieved document is not evidence that a particular user action is appropriate.

## From a resource to an accepted finding

1. The central orchestrator gives each worker a bounded shared Twin snapshot and its own registered skill names.

2. Workers calculate from dated NAV, disclosure, benchmark and statement evidence. Missing identity, dates and costs stay unknown. Sources do not become user facts.

3. The MF findings enter the same grounded-review selection and final priority ranking as equity, FD, tax and goal findings. Benchmark review requires multiple matched windows and rolling evidence. Overlap and return correlation remain separate.

4. Each finding carries facts, arithmetic when applicable, exact project-authored skill-section excerpts, whole-skill and section hashes, applicable source IDs, limitations and alternatives. The central validator rejects changed or missing excerpts, altered proof digests and unsupported arithmetic.

5. A language model can explain accepted evidence within the supported contract. It cannot supply a new return, rate, tax liability, scheme identity, probability or citation. Book/framework titles never become an outcome guarantee.

6. Cross-agent feasibility keeps one budget and checks constraints after the parallel workers join. Current-year realized tax is not reused as a prospective MF-switch estimate. Unverified scheme classification, dated lots, redemption scope or exit terms withhold transaction amounts. FD alternatives reuse the same dated official catalogue as FIRE, with eligibility and renewal limits visible.

## Application screening policy

The MF benchmark review currently requires at least two distinct supported trailing windows plus at least 12 rolling observations. The negative direction requires rolling outperformance below 50% and negative median excess return; the positive direction requires supported trailing excess and positive median rolling excess. These windows overlap and are not independent statistical trials. The 50% disclosed-overlap review threshold and small/mid-cap versus effective-risk-score screen are also application policy, not legal limits or trade instructions.

The tests use synthetic funds and verify that the MF evidence changes the final ranked decision, that unsupported single-period signals are rejected, that actual Twin scores govern suitability, and that incomplete costs never produce fabricated savings.


## Automatic published PPFAS comparisons

The narrow PPFAS adapter supports the two tested Direct Growth ISINs for Flexi Cap and ELSS Tax Saver. It validates the official HTTPS monthly URL, exact scheme panel and title, unique NAV observation date, NIFTY 500 TRI benchmark label and the specific Fund Performance table's Direct/Regular columns. Adjacent SIP and rolling tables cannot substitute for trailing returns. Schema changes fail closed.

The AMC's published plan table does not expressly label Growth/IDCW or give exact starting market dates. The response retains those limitations and keeps the reported 1/3/5-year comparison separate from recomputed NAV and TRI history. It does not manufacture a benchmark chart, rolling consistency, tracking error or a switch signal. The scheme benchmark identity can link a separately supplied authorised TRI series.

[Live source receipt](validation/ppfas-published-comparisons.json) records the September 2026 plan comparison, including observation date and source hash. Published figures remain historical facts, not future return inputs. The fetcher uses the existing public cache, tries at most three fixed monthly URLs and respects the provider's shared network budget.
