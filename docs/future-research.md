# Future Research — AI-CIO

> Status: **PLANNED / NOT IMPLEMENTED**. Research directions only.

## Physiological and robotic behavioural sensing

A future research direction is to augment the psychometric profile with physiological signals measured while the user responds to financial scenarios.

Potential signals:

- Heart rate
- Heart rate variability (HRV)
- Galvanic skin response (GSR)

These signals could be captured during scenario questions, market simulations, or portfolio reviews to provide additional behavioural context.

## Caveats

- Physiological signals are **never direct proof of investment suitability** or risk tolerance.
- Any sensing requires explicit user consent.
- Privacy controls must be designed before collection.
- Sensors require per-user calibration.
- Signal noise must be handled and documented.
- Scientific validation is required before any signal is used in decision support.
- Uncertainty must be explicit in any output that uses these signals.

None of this is implemented in the MVP.
