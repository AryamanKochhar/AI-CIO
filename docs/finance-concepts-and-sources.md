# Financial concepts from primary sources

Reviewed 20 October 2026. Six additional public sources inform four original applied skills and six retrievable notes. The application loads these skills and source metadata; registered exact excerpts can appear in the existing verifiable review proofs. Missing facts remain missing. No full books, gated content or publisher articles are bundled.

| Concept | First principle | Review implication |
|---|---|---|
| Real return | Wealth growth must be compared with consumption-price growth over the same period. | Establish inflation and nominal/net conventions before calling a corpus adequate. |
| Sequence risk | Withdrawals change the base on which later returns act. | An accumulation curve does not establish retirement withdrawal safety. |
| Bond price | Present value discounts promised future cashflows. | Coupon, yield and realized return differ; review credit, rate, call and liquidity risks. |
| Fee drag | Charges remove future compounding capital. | Inspect disclosed costs; do not double-deduct costs or infer savings when fees are unknown. |
| Capacity and consent | Income minus outgoings is available cash, not a chosen investment amount. | The explicit monthly investment-budget field is currently absent. Surplus-funded paths are unconfirmed illustrations. |
| Diversification | Joint asset movements affect portfolio variation. | More holdings do not establish adequate diversification; missing look-through limits conclusions. |
| Forecast evidence | A conditional scenario is not a calibrated likelihood. | Relative review support and market indicators cannot establish success probabilities. |
