# FIRE research and planning evidence

Verified on **20 October 2026 (Asia/Kolkata)**. The source register is [fire_sources.json](../knowledge/fire_sources.json); the applied guide is [fire-planning/SKILL.md](../.cursor/skills/fire-planning/SKILL.md). These original notes explain a goal, its calculations and the limits of the evidence. They do not claim to reproduce complete books or provide certified personal investment advice.

## Start with the spending obligation

Financial independence means that a funding plan can support the spending the person intends to maintain. A universal 25-times-spending figure hides inflation, lifespan, taxes, income timing and investment risks. At a target age of 65, the product should say financial independence or retirement by 65; early retirement depends on the person's own intended working life.

[SEBI's retirement guidance](https://investor.sebi.gov.in/moneymatters-planforearlyretire.html) discusses lifestyle, emergency provision, insurance and diversification. Its [inflation explanation](https://investor.sebi.gov.in/moneymatters-inflation.html) distinguishes money from purchasing power. [NISM's public retirement objectives](https://www.nism.ac.in/test-objectives-retirement-adviser) identify retirement income, horizon, real and after-tax returns, existing funding, shortfalls and review. These public pages support the planning questions; they do not prescribe the application's allocation bands.

The project derives annual target-date spending as monthly spending today times 12 times an explicit inflation factor. Subtract reliable income only after aligning its start date and rupee basis. Divide the resulting annual portfolio need by a selected withdrawal fraction to display a scenario corpus. A finite-horizon annuity calculation answers a different question and consumes capital. Both need disclosed assumptions.

## Treat withdrawal research as context

[Morningstar's 2025 original report](https://www.morningstar.com/content/cs-assets/v3/assets/blt9415ea4cc4157833/bltb73b87c5d0c70ead/692f43f57737a31596684522/working_file_11.19_FINAL_REVISE.pdf) uses US-market assumptions. Its 30-year base case, spending rule and model success criterion are explicit. Longer retirement horizons and poor early returns change the result. We do not transfer its rate or model probability into a guaranteed Indian FIRE outcome. The application's constant-return saving scenarios do not simulate withdrawal sequence risk.

## Use preferences as an actual constraint

[CFA's suitability guidance](https://www.cfainstitute.org/standards/professionals/code-ethics-standards/standards-of-practice-iii-c) calls for portfolio context, objectives and constraints. Its [risk-tolerance research](https://rpc.cfainstitute.org/research/foundation/2018/risk-tolerance-and-circumstances) explains psychological willingness and circumstances. This supports keeping risk willingness distinct from financial loss capacity.

The planner must show which psychometric result it used and how that changes the proposed mix or alternative. A short horizon or reserve gap can constrain a high declared risk appetite. A funding shortfall must not overrule a conservative preference. Numerical score cutoffs, portfolio percentages, stress shocks and expected returns remain disclosed application policies.

Contribution willingness is a separate monthly amount. The planner should use the lower of this amount and uncommitted monthly surplus, protect other goals, and deduct each EMI once. Missing willingness is not agreement to invest all surplus. A zero amount is valid. Existing assets count only when explicitly earmarked and available for this goal.

Minimum deposit and resident senior eligibility are linked separately in each product record: [SBI terms](https://sbi.bank.in/web/personal-banking/investments-deposits/deposits/fixed-deposit), [SBI senior terms](https://sbi.bank.in/documents/16012/396762/151024-Facilities%20for%20Senior%20Citizens%20updated%20August%202024.pdf), [HDFC regular FD](https://www.hdfc.bank.in/fixed-deposit/regular-fixed-deposits) and [ICICI FAQs](https://www.icici.bank.in/personal-banking/deposits/fixed-deposit/fixed-deposit-faqs). SBI's senior premium also has an INR 10000 minimum and primary-holder condition. A current 60-year eligibility test cannot be replaced by the person's future age of 65.

An FD is a lump-sum contract. Monthly money assigned to an FD bucket must be accumulated or booked later on then-available terms; it is not a promise of monthly purchases at today's rate. Early closure can reduce interest. Tax, actual day count, compounding and payout selection affect proceeds. No after-tax yield or maturity amount is manufactured.

[DICGC's official guide](https://www.dicgc.org.in/guide-to-deposit-insurance) limits eligible cover to INR 500000 including interest, aggregating accounts at a bank in the same right and capacity. Missing existing account balances prevent an available-cover calculation. The catalog's seven-day review window is a freshness policy; it does not guarantee a bank will leave a rate unchanged for seven days.

## What a proof can establish

A useful explanation connects the recorded facts, arithmetic, exact project skill section and conditional next action. Exact source verification establishes that the quoted project text is present. It cannot verify personal facts or prove a future investment outcome. A smooth projected path is not a probability, and a ranking percentage is not a success rate. The skill's seven registered principles preserve those boundaries.
